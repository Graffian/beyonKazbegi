BEYOND KAZBEGI — DEVELOPER BRIEF
1. PROJECT VISION

Build a premium, extremely fast, SEO-focused tourism and adventure platform for all of Georgia.

The website should NOT feel like a traditional tour agency website.

It should feel like a combination of:

GetYourGuide + Airbnb Experiences + local concierge + AI travel planner

but focused specifically on Georgia.

Main goal:

A visitor should be able to find almost any experience in Georgia, understand it, select their preferences and send a structured reservation request through WhatsApp in less than 60 seconds.

Brand:

BEYOND KAZBEGI

Long-term positioning:

Explore Georgia Beyond the Ordinary.

Even though the company started in Kazbegi, the platform must be architected from day one to support all regions of Georgia.
